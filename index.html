Easy to Use

How to Prepare
Your Sea Moss

01

Rinse

Rinse your dried sea moss under water to remove sea debris and remove any visible debris

02

Wash

Cut key limes and squeeze all over sea moss and thoroughly wash seamoss and rinse, repeat if needed

03

Boil

Take a pot, add sea moss, add fresh spring water. Make sure sea moss is completely submerged and covered in water. Let boil for 10-20 minutes

04

Cool & Refrigerate

Let cool and blend in blender until you get the desired smooth consistency. Add to mason jar and refrigerate for gel to form. Discard gel after 30 days

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked
Questions

Honduras wild-crafted sea moss grows in pristine ocean conditions, absorbing a full spectrum of minerals from the water. It's considered some of the cleanest, most mineral-dense sea moss available — no pool-grown shortcuts here.

Dried sea moss has a shelf life of 1–2 years when stored in a cool, dry place. Once you make gel, it lasts 3–4 weeks in the refrigerator or up to 6 months in the freezer.

We recommend eating 1–2 tablespoons of sea moss gel per day. You can add it to smoothies, juices, soups, oatmeal, or take it directly.

You can purchase directly on Walmart.com (fulfilled by Walmart, eligible for free shipping) or through our TikTok Shop.
